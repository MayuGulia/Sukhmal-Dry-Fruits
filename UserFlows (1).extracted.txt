11. Cross-Cutting Edge Cases (apply
across all flows)
Scenario 	Required Behavior
Session expires mid-checkout
Save cart/form state, prompt re-login,
return user to exact same checkout step
— never lose entered data
Item goes out of stock while in cart
Flag at Cart and again at Checkout (re-
validate stock right before payment, not
just at add-to-cart time)

Show updated price with a clear notice,

Idempotent order creation — same

Merge local guest data into account data,

as unpaid indefinitely
